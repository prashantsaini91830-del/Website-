Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

On this page

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Gyaan Hub collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using the site you confirm that you have read it.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, and your display name and profile details if you sign in with Google.
  • Authentication data: sign-in timestamps and security identifiers handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Usage data (only if you allow analytics): pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected through Google Analytics.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep your session secure.
  • To show you the study materials you are entitled to access.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it (analytics, with your consent).

3. Cookies and local storage

Essential: used to keep you signed in and to remember your consent choice. These cannot be switched off.

Analytics: optional. Enabled only if you accept them in the consent popup. You can change your choice any time from the menu or from Settings.

4. Third-party services

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Analytics) to process data on our behalf. Their handling of data is described in Google's own privacy documentation.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal data. We keep account data while your account is active and delete it when you ask us to, unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

7. Security

We use industry-standard services to protect your data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Gyaan Hub is intended for students. If you are under the age required by your local law to consent on your own, please use the site with a parent or guardian's permission.

9. Contact

For privacy requests, contact: your-email@example.com (replace with your real contact address).
